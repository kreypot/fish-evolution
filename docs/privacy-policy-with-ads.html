Privacy Policy — Fish Evolution: Ocean Run

Effective date: (put the date you release the version with ads)

Summary

Fish Evolution: Ocean Run does not ask you for an account and does not collect personal information itself. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google's advertising software may collect and use some data on your device as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show:

  • an ad when the game starts (not on your first launches),
  • a full-screen ad between levels, and
  • optional rewarded videos that give you bonus coins.

To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived),
  • information about how you interact with ads and the app, and
  • diagnostic information such as crashes and performance.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More about how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive this personal data. AdMob gives us only summary reports such as the number of ads shown and earnings.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are used. In those regions you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad Privacy Settings in the game.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your Android settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Rewarded videos are always optional.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocked levels, best scores), your coins and the fish and designs you have unlocked, how many times you started the game, and your settings in a small file in the game's private storage on your device. We do not receive this file. You can erase it with Settings → Reset Level Progress (levels only) or by uninstalling the game.

Permissions

The game uses the internet and network state permissions only so that ads can load, and the advertising ID permission so that Google can show relevant ads. The game works fully offline; without internet no ads are shown.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing or by watching optional ads and cannot be bought with real money.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third parties

  • Google AdMob (advertising), see above.
  • The open-source Godot Engine (MIT licence) runs on your device and is not configured to send data anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
