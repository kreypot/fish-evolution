Privacy Policy — Fish Evolution: Ocean Run

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Summary

Fish Evolution: Ocean Run does not collect, store, transmit, or share any information from your device or about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game:

  • does not collect personal information (name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, or device identifiers);
  • does not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking tools of any kind;
  • does not contain ads or third-party SDKs;
  • does not require an account or sign-in;
  • has no in-app purchases: coins are earned only by playing and cannot be bought with real money;
  • does not connect to the internet or to any server. It is fully playable offline;
  • does not request any Android permissions.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocked levels, best scores), your coins and the fish designs you have unlocked, and your settings (music and sound on/off, whether you have seen the tutorial) in a small file in the game's private storage on your device. This file never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset Progress or by uninstalling the game.

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and no links that leave the game.

Third parties

The game is built with the open-source Godot Engine (MIT licence). The engine runs entirely on your device and is not configured to send data anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. A release that collects data would require your explicit consent first.
