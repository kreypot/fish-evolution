## 2. Tags (Grow > Store presence > Store settings > App category > Manage tags)
You cannot type your own tags. You pick **up to 5** from Google's list (about 150 tags) and Google shows suggestions.
Set Application type = **Game**, Category = **Arcade** (or Casual), then search the tag list for these words and pick the 5 closest that exist:
1. Arcade
2. Casual
3. Offline (or "No internet required")
4. Animals (or Fish / Underwater, if present)
5. Single player (or Survival / Simulation, whichever fits best)
Only choose tags that clearly describe the game. Tags that describe other apps can hurt your ranking.
Changes take up to 24 hours to show. Don't keep changing them.

## 4. Do NOT do these (Google can reject or demote the app)
- Don't put other games' names (Hungry Shark, Feed and Grow, Fish Eat Fish, etc.) anywhere in the listing.
- Don't stuff keywords in the title or repeat the same word many times.
- Don't use emojis, ALL CAPS or words like "best", "#1" or "free" in the app name.
- Don't claim features the game doesn't have.

## 5. What really moves you up the search results
1. **Installs and retention:** how many people install it and keep playing. Your 12+ testers help a little; real players matter most.
2. **Ratings and reviews:** ask friends to rate honestly (4.5+ helps a lot). Reply to every review.
3. **Listing conversion:** the icon, the first 2 screenshots, and a short gameplay video (YouTube link) decide whether people install.
   Put a caption on each screenshot, like "Eat fish to grow!", "300 levels", "Customize your fish".
4. **Translations:** add listings in Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Hindi, Indonesian, Arabic, Russian, French and German.
   Games are searched in many languages and there is far less competition than in English.
5. **Updates:** release improvements every few weeks. Google favors apps that stay active.
6. **Low crash rate:** check Play Console > Android vitals regularly.
